# SPMB 2027/2028 runs in the portal; the brochure is its data

> Decided by the user on 2026-10-02, from the yayasan's brochure
> *BROSUR CIPANSOR 2027-2028 (REVISI)* and its letterheads. The research
> behind each choice is below; do not repeat it.

## What was decided

1. **Registration for 2027/2028 runs in the portal and on the public site**
   (`/public/spmb`). There is no bit.ly link. The brochure's link points to a
   separate site (pesantrencipansor.com, built by a vendor), where registration
   has been open since 1 October 2026. The portal must be released to
   production to take over (a release still needs the user's approval). The
   realistic target is before wave 2 opens on **1 January 2027**.
2. **The brochure's data goes into the SPMB module, not into code.** An admin
   enters it each year, and the public page and the chatbot read it from there
   (golden rule 8). The module gains:
   - per wave, the four sessions: registration, test, results, re-registration;
   - per wave, a discount for paying in full, with units excluded (the brochure
     excludes TK Qur'an);
   - a fee breakdown per unit × ikhwan/akhwat × boarding/non-boarding (infaq
     bangunan, ranjang–kasur–lemari, seragam, buku, infaq pendidikan, the
     registration fee, the monthly SPP);
   - per unit, the requirements (documents) and the minimum age;
   - per unit, a contact person and number, entered by the admin and kept in
     the database, never in the repository. The call centre stays the main
     contact.
3. **Requirements follow the brochure**, as the user chose:
   - SD IT: at least 7 years old, a copy of the TK certificate, a selection
     test;
   - TK Qur'an: at least 5 years old.

   **Known risk, stated when the user chose:** Permendikdasmen 3/2025 Pasal 11
   says otherwise:
   - 7 is the priority age, 6 on 1 July is enough, and 5 years 6 months with a
     psychologist's or the teachers' recommendation;
   - no test may be required for SD grade 1;
   - no TK certificate is required.

   Pasal 10 sets TK A at 4–5 years and TK B at 5–6. Whether these articles bind
   a private school is not stated in so many words; secondary sources say they
   do. Because the requirements are data (point 2), changing them later is an
   admin's edit, not a release.
4. **The Ketua's name is "H. Ramram Mansur Ramdani"**, as on the signed tahfidz
   certificate and the public site. "Ramran" in the brochure is a misprint.
5. **The public site shows the full structure, names and positions:**
   - 3 Pembina, 3 Pengawas, the Pengurus Harian;
   - the pesantren's own structure: Pimpinan, Sekretaris, Bendahara, Direktur
     Tahfidz Ikhwan/Akhwat, Kepala TK Qur'an, Kepengasuhan Ikhwan/Akhwat.

   It is in three languages. Photos appear only where one already exists, and
   no phone numbers are shown. These are positions held by people, in the
   organisation tree of `struktur-organisasi-dan-identitas.md` — not new role
   codes.
6. **Each unit has an official name and a short name.** Documents (naskah
   dinas letterhead, rapor, certificates, SPMB) print the official name; menus
   and screens keep the short one. Each unit also stores its NPSN, operating
   permit number and email.

   - Accreditation in the reference data: SD IT A, SMP IT B, SMA Qur'an A. A

## Misprints to pass back to the yayasan

- The footer reads "pesantrencipansor.om".
- "Lulusan SMA Pesantren Cipansor" should name SMA Qur'an.
- "Genarasi" is misspelled.
- Wave 2's date range is missing its dash.
- Wave 3: the test and results fall before registration closes (31 Mei).
- Wave 4 has no test, results or re-registration dates.
- "Tes seleksi PPDB" and "Penerimaan Peserta Didik Baru" should read SPMB.
- A WhatsApp announcement says 2026/2027.
- Takhosus is spelled two ways.
- The Ketua's name is misspelled (point 4).
